About me

Rishabh Bohra.

I’m a B.Tech student at IET DAVV. I work on mobile apps and embedded systems, and I’m exploring where AI fits into tools people actually use.

Connected devices

Scoot started with a question about what a phone and a scooter could share over Bluetooth. With esp-reddit, the challenge was fitting an API client into the resources of an ESP32.

Applied AI

In Exam Evaluation, I worked on bringing rubric-based grading and faculty review into one flow. detroit-jev explores a different question: how a model’s decisions hold together over a long story.

Open source

I’ve also worked on a Home Assistant HomeKit integration fix. You can find my public projects and contributions on GitHub.
